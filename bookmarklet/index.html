WebPenPresenter

Point, highlight and write on any web page while you share your screen. Your students see exactly what you mean.

Made for teaching from an iPad with an Apple Pencil. It also works on a computer with a mouse. You set it up once, in about five minutes, and after that it is one tap away on every page.

Set it up on the iPad (once)

The tool lives inside a bookmark. You will save this page as a bookmark, then replace its address with a small piece of code. Here is how.

  1. Save this page as a bookmark. Tap the Share button at the top of Safari (the square with an arrow pointing up), then tap Add Bookmark. Name it Presenter and tap Save.
  2. Copy the code. Tap the button below. That is all; the code is now copied.
    Show the code
  3. Paste the code into the bookmark. Tap the book icon to open your bookmarks. Tap Edit at the bottom, then tap the Presenter bookmark. Tap the address line (the one that starts with https), tap it once more and choose Select All, then Paste. The address now starts with javascript:. Tap Done twice.

Tip: in Settings, Safari, turn on Show Favorites Bar. The Presenter bookmark then sits right under the address bar on every page.

Set it up on a computer

In Chrome or Edge, show the bookmarks bar (press Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac). Then drag this red button onto that bar:

Presenter

If dragging does not work, right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page, name it Presenter and paste the code from above as the address.

Use it in class

  1. Open the website you want to show and start sharing your screen.
  2. Tap the Presenter bookmark. A small bar appears at the bottom left of the page.
  3. Draw with the pencil. Your fingers still scroll and tap the page as usual.

Laser: a red dot follows the pencil. Write with it and the note stays for a moment, then fades away on its own. A quick tap presses whatever is under the dot, so you can click buttons with the pencil too.

Mark: a yellow highlighter. Pen: a red or blue line. Both stay until you tap Clear.

Red / Blue and Thick change the pen. Erase rubs out part of a line. Undo takes back the last stroke.

Finger: lets fingers (or the mouse) draw too. While it is on, the page cannot be tapped.

Hide: puts the bar away. A small Pointer button stays to bring it back.

After you reload the page or open another site, tap the bookmark again.

If it does not work

  • Nothing happens on some sites. A few sites refuse outside tools: GitHub, Wikipedia, YouTube, Google Docs and Slides, GeoGebra. For those, use the Chrome extension on a computer. News sites, Desmos, Khan Academy, Canvas and Excalidraw all work.
  • It only works on web pages. Not on the home screen, in other apps, or on a PDF opened directly in Safari.
  • Inside an embedded video or calculator the pencil reaches the embed, not the overlay. The extension handles that; the bookmark does not.

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you draw or look at is sent anywhere. Privacy.

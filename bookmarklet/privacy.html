WebPen Presenter privacy

WebPen Presenter, as a bookmark or as a browser extension, draws on top of the page you are looking at. That is all it does.

  • It collects no personal data, no browsing history and no page content.
  • It sends nothing to any server. The bookmark downloads the script from this site and the script then runs entirely in your browser; the extension ships the script inside the package and downloads nothing.
  • Your tool settings (laser or pen, color, thickness) are kept in the browser tab's session storage so they survive a reload, and vanish when the tab closes.
  • The extension asks to run on all sites because you choose, at presentation time, which site to draw over. It does nothing visible on a page until you press its toolbar button or keyboard shortcut.
